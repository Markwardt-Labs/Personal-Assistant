# Events

`AgendaService`, `CalendarAi`, `EventSuggestionsAi`, `StoredCalendar`, `StoredSuggestions`, `StoredFeeds` and the events tab of the client (`EventsViewModel` and its views).

## What the server keeps

The calendar part is read by the AI, in a session of its own that has the Google Calendar connector and is deleted again, over a window from the first day of the previous month to the end of the next one (three months). It lists the events of every calendar the user has (declined ones left out) with their identifier, title, start and end, location, a short note and attendees. The server keeps only events of the window that have an identifier and do not end before they start, once per identifier, in `calendar-cache.json`, and shows that until the next read. A read takes a few minutes, so it runs when the calendar was never read, once a day with the suggestions, on refresh, and after the assistant changed an event in the chat (`RefreshCalendar`, which does not search for suggestions); a failed read keeps the older events and puts the reason (or the problem the AI reported, such as a connector that is not connected) in the `CalendarError` of the state, and is not repeated until the next refresh or the daily run. The suggestions are what the AI found for the event feeds, kept in `agenda-suggestions.json`, and the feeds are kept in `event-feeds.json`. A feed has a name (1 to 40 characters, unique without regard to case) and a description of what to look for (3 to 300 characters); at most ten exist.

## Searching for suggestions

The AI searches the web in a session of its own for the next 30 days near the location the user set in the app, for the feeds only, and is told when the user is busy from the calendar as last read (times only, no titles or people) so it can prefer events that do not clash. Every draft must name an existing feed, carry an http or https address and start inside the period; the same title at the same time appears once, at most eight suggestions are kept per feed, and the list is ordered by start. The search runs once a day through `DailySchedule`, on refresh (every feed), and when a feed is added: that search covers the new feed alone, with the calendar as it is, and the suggestions of the other feeds are kept as they were, so adding a feed costs one feed of searching. With no feed nothing is searched and no error is raised; with feeds but no location the state carries an error that says to add it in the settings of the app. Removing a feed removes its suggestions at once, and the state leaves out suggestions that are over.

## The client

The tab shows one of three views of the same data. The day view lists one day and the week view the seven days from the one shown (today at first, not a calendar week; moving goes by seven days), each with the calendar and the suggestions, all-day events first and then by start time; an event that crosses midnight is on every day it covers, and all-day events use their dates as the calendar gives them instead of the time zone of the phone. The month view is a grid of six weeks starting on the first day of the week of the culture, with a count of events and a count of suggestions on each day; tapping a day opens it. Moving by day, week or month beyond the loaded months works but says that events are loaded only for those months. Tapping an entry opens its details (venue, description, the feed it relates to, a button for its page, like and dislike for suggestions).
